# Ioana Orca Coaching — Pre-Launch “Coming Soon” Page

## Overall direction

The coming-soon page should not feel like a generic “my website is launching” page.

It should already communicate the core of Ioana’s brand:

**I know something needs to change → I understand what’s keeping me stuck → I get clear on what I want → I’m ready to act.**

The page should speak especially to women who are in an **identity shift / new chapter** but feel blocked by an old belief, fear, guilt, resentment or uncertainty.

---

## 1. Logo / Name

**IOANA ORCA**

_Coaching for your next chapter._

---

## 2. Main Headline

### Recommended direction

**You know something needs to change.  
You just don’t know what to do next.**

Supporting text:

> I help women understand what’s keeping them stuck, get clear on what they actually want, and make decisions they feel confident about.

This should be the main emotional entry point: recognition first, then clarity about what Ioana does.

---

## 3. “This Might Be You” Section

> Maybe you’re thinking about leaving your job.  
> Starting something of your own.  
> Ending or changing a relationship.  
> Finally showing your ideas to the world.  
> Or simply admitting that the life that used to work for you… doesn’t anymore.
>
> You’re not necessarily lost.  
> You might just be in the middle of becoming someone who wants something different.

This speaks directly to the identity-shift pattern that has appeared across client work.

---

## 4. What You Can Expect

Keep this section simple and structured.

### Understand yourself

See the patterns, beliefs and perceptions influencing your decisions.

### Get clear

Separate what _you_ actually want from what you think you _should_ want.

### Move forward

Make a conscious decision and take the next step.

---

## 5. Method / Credibility

Don’t make Demartini the headline. Let the human problem and desired transformation lead.

### 1:1 Coaching | Demartini Method

> My work combines coaching with the Demartini Method to help you explore the perceptions, emotional patterns and beliefs that can keep you stuck.
>
> No endless analysing. No telling you what you _should_ do.  
> We work towards understanding — and then making a choice.

---

## 6. Coming Soon / CTA

Since this is a pre-launch page, the CTA should focus on being first rather than selling something that isn’t ready yet.

> **Something new is coming.**
>
> I’m building a space for women who are ready to understand themselves better, make clearer decisions and create their next chapter.
>
> The website is launching soon.
>
> **Want to know when it’s live?**

### CTA

**[ JOIN THE LIST ]**

Alternative if there is no email list yet:

**Follow me on Instagram @ioanaorca**

---

## 7. Personal Element

A short introduction can make the page feel human and personal:

> **Hi, I’m Ioana.**
>
> I know what it feels like to realise that the life you built isn’t necessarily the life you want next.
>
> I’ve changed countries, careers, relationships, plans and directions. And I’ve learned that understanding yourself isn’t about finding what’s wrong with you.
>
> It’s about understanding yourself well enough to make different choices.

The personal story should support the reader rather than become a biography.

# Strategic Goal

Even before the full website launches, the page should make it immediately clear:

- **Who Ioana is**
- **Who she helps**
- **What problem she helps with**
- **What changes through her work**
- **How she works**
- **What the visitor should do next**

The page should also reinforce the future-facing direction of the brand: not endless self-analysis, but **understanding → choice → action → creating the next chapter**.
